01 / The Intersection

I work at the seam between building and measuring.

Most people pick a lane. I've spent my time learning to live in the overlap — the place where a product idea has to survive contact with real data, and where a metric only matters if it changes what a team decides to ship.

At TikTok, I owned policy and enforcement systems for LIVE, translating fuzzy trust-and-safety goals into measurable moderation outcomes. At Astreas, I built the data pipelines and cleaning logic that made wearable health analytics trustworthy in the first place.

The thread connecting it all: I like turning messy, ambiguous problems into systems that are testable, measurable, and shippable — and I like doing it alongside engineers, designers, and operators.
